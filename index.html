How to play

Start with a six-task process and no stations. Open Total process to edit task times and use the Network or Yamazumi view to assign tasks. Cut and Drill both precede Glue; then come Fasten, Paint, and Package. Place predecessor tasks at the same or an earlier station.

Add stations on the canvas for $40 each. Each starts without a worker. You begin with three hired workers; assign them using a station's gear. Every hired worker costs $4 per day.

Raw goods holds 10 units; add 5 spaces for $2. WIP queues hold 5 units; add one space for $2. A full queue blocks upstream work.

A breakdown or absence pauses before production so you can adjust your plan. Bad parts are revealed after the shift and returned to Raw goods for rework. Each event has an independent 15% chance.

Ship 30 units by day 10. Inventory costs $1 per unit per day. Finished goods sell for $10 each, up to daily demand.
